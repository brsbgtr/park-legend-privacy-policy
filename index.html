01 Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to PARK LEGEND: Parking Puzzle (the “Application”), a mobile game offered by Zenitra Games (the “Service Provider”). It explains how information may be collected, used, shared, retained, and deleted when you use the Application, including through integrated third-party services.

02 Information collection

When you use the Application, information processed by the Application and its integrated services may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate location inferred from IP address;
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or application identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • Device model, operating system, app version, language, and technical diagnostics;
  • App interactions, session length, and usage information associated with advertising or service functionality.

Some information is collected automatically by third-party SDKs rather than directly entered by you. The categories actually collected depend on the SDKs enabled, your device settings, permissions, region, and consent choices.

03 How information is used

Information may be processed to operate the Application, deliver and measure advertising (including optional rewarded ads), diagnose technical problems, prevent fraud or misuse, and comply with applicable law. Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable legal requirements and your choices.

05 Advertising, cookies & choices

The Application is ad-supported and may display interstitial ads between gameplay moments and optional rewarded ads selected by the player. Advertising SDKs may use identifiers and similar technologies to deliver, limit, and measure ads. Where required, consent will be requested before relevant data processing occurs.

You may be able to manage ad personalization through your device privacy settings and consent choices provided in the Application. Declining optional tracking, where applicable, does not prevent use of the core game. Uninstalling the Application stops future activity by the app on your device but does not automatically erase data already received by third parties.

06 Sharing & international transfers

Information may be shared with service providers involved in advertising, technical operations, measurement, and fraud prevention, subject to their respective terms and applicable law. Information may also be disclosed to comply with legal obligations or protect users and services. Some providers may process data in countries other than your country of residence; any required transfer safeguards apply according to relevant law and provider arrangements.

07 Retention & deletion

Zenitra Games aims to retain any personal information it directly controls only for as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law. Third-party service providers apply their own retention schedules, which are described in their privacy policies. Data that is no longer needed should be deleted or anonymized when appropriate.

To request access to or deletion of personal information held by Zenitra Games, email zenitragames@gmail.com with the subject “Park Legend Privacy Request”. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. For information controlled by independent third-party providers, you may also need to use those providers’ privacy controls or contact them directly.

08 Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law (including GDPR or California privacy laws), you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information; object to certain processing; or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have rights relating to the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. We will handle eligible requests and communicate applicable options through zenitragames@gmail.com.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal. You may have the right to complain to a relevant data protection authority.

09 Children's privacy

The Application is not designed to knowingly solicit personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where age-specific rules or parental consent are required, Zenitra Games will seek to apply the appropriate restrictions and safeguards. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact zenitragames@gmail.com so the concern can be reviewed.

The legal age for consent to certain data processing varies by country and is not the same as the game's content age rating.

10 Security

Zenitra Games seeks to use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to information under its control. No internet transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Any notifications concerning a reportable data breach will be provided as required by applicable law.

11 Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Application, SDK integrations, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Where legally required, additional notice or consent will be provided.
